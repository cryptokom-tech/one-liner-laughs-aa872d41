# Candidates — One-Liner Laughs

Judged locally against the kit brief, in order: (1) real one-line joke
with setup and payoff, (2) laugh out loud, (3) surprise turn,
(4) freshness, (5) economy. Field study (268 entries at build time):
GPS "recalculating", smart-fridge snack judging, password-manager
amnesia, smart-speaker bank notifications, calendar self-meetings,
dying plants and quitting AI assistants are all saturated — every
candidate below avoids those angles.

## Chosen

`I joined a book club to meet people; we never meet, which is exactly how I like people.`

87 chars. Setup promises sociability, payoff reverses it twice: the
club never meets, and that failure is the point. Fresh angle (no
book-club line in the visible field), punch lands on the last word.

## Runners-up

- `I asked my landlord for a rent reduction; he offered to let me keep the mice.` (77) — strong laugh, but a landlord angle already appears in the field.
- `I asked the museum guard which exhibit was priceless; he pointed at the exit.` (77) — clean surprise, slightly dry laugh.
- `I taught my parrot to say "we need to talk"; now even the bird sounds disappointed in me.` (89) — funny image, payoff softer than the chosen line.
- `My goldfish has a three-second memory and still remembers to judge me.` (70) — tightest of the set, but the three-second-memory premise is well worn.
